# Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

A log of significant architectural decisions, tradeoffs, and gotchas discovered along the way.

---

## ADR Template

```
## ADR-NNNN: <Title>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-XXXX

### Context
What situation or problem prompted this decision?

### Decision
What was decided?

### Consequences
What are the tradeoffs, risks, or follow-on work?
```
